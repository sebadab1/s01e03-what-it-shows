Problems

The reports split into two causes. The intake tag is only a guess; the tenant locale, booking form, and channel determine the cause.

Shared parser reads dates month first

For tenants whose locale writes the day before the month, the shared parser flips day and month. This cause appears across app, email, and booking-v2 form reports. The reports in this group are tracked in the shared parser ticket.

booking-v3 hint conflicts with day-first locales

The booking-v3 form tells people to enter month first, while its tenant locale reads dates day first. This separate form-hint problem affects booking-v3 form reports only. The reports are tracked in the booking-v3 hint ticket; do not merge it with the shared parser issue.
